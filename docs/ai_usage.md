## What AI Did NOT Do

- **AI did not run any experiments.** All numerical results (RMSE, MAE, R², etc.)
  come from actual Python execution by the team.
- **AI did not fabricate results.** Every number in every document matches
  the actual notebook outputs.
- **AI did not make scientific conclusions on behalf of the team.** The analysis
  and hypothesis assessments reflect the team's interpretation of the actual evidence.
- **AI did not change the experimental methodology.** The notebook code, feature
  definitions, model hyperparameters, and evaluation approach were designed by the team.

---

## How AI Outputs Were Verified

1. The team ran the notebook (`notebooks/Project_Code.ipynb`) from top to bottom
   and confirmed all outputs match the documented results.
2. The team reviewed all markdown cell explanations in the notebook for accuracy.
3. The team cross-checked all numbers in `docs/results_interpretation.md`
   against the actual notebook cell outputs.
4. The team verified that the research question, hypotheses, and conclusions
   in `docs/investigation.md` accurately reflect the experimental design.

## Examples of Team Modifications to AI Suggestions

- The DATA_PATH in the notebook was adapted to use `os.getcwd()` with a fallback
  absolute path, based on the team's actual file system setup.
